## Status rules

- `DIRECT_USER` only when the participant did advance, reimbursement or vendor-payment work in the last 6 months in a company of up to about 200 staff (form questions 4–7).
- `PROXY` remains a separate evidence class.
- `PILOT_NOT_COUNTED` is never counted in DIRECT_USER or PROXY totals.
- Aim for a mix: requesters, at least one approver and at least one accountant.
- Method must be `MODERATED` or `ASYNC_SELF_REPORT`.
- `COMPLETED` requires consent, exact build reference and D-01 through D-05 completed in the canonical form.
- `VERIFIED_RECORD` requires an anonymized artifact plus integrity review.
- MODERATED may support observed-behavior/task-outcome evidence.
- ASYNC_SELF_REPORT remains self-report; do not infer observed task success, timing or moderator help.
- A public “interested” reply is recruitment interest, not a participant record.
- Do not claim iteration improvement from one participant.
