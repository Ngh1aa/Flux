# Flux Round 01 — Screener & Consent

## Study fit

**Internal purpose:** Validate comprehension and recovery around approval ownership, role permissions, settlement failure and audit trace.  
**Do not reveal desired answers or preferred design direction to candidates.**

## Candidate questions

1. In the past 6 months, how often have you prepared, reviewed, approved or monitored business payments?
   - [ ] Never
   - [ ] Once
   - [ ] Occasionally (monthly or less)
   - [ ] Frequently (weekly or more)

2. Which tasks have you personally done? Select all that apply.
   - [ ] Prepare supplier/vendor payments
   - [ ] Review or approve payments
   - [ ] Manage treasury/liquidity or bank balances
   - [ ] Resolve failed/returned payments
   - [ ] Configure or follow approval policies
   - [ ] Reconcile/audit payment history
   - [ ] None of these

3. What is your usual role in those tasks?
   - [ ] Primary operator/preparer
   - [ ] Reviewer/approver
   - [ ] Finance manager/controller/CFO
   - [ ] Accountant/bookkeeper
   - [ ] Adjacent operations/compliance/procurement role
   - [ ] Observer/support only

4. Which tools/services do you currently use for these tasks?  
   ______

5. Are there accessibility, assistive-technology, language, device, zoom, connectivity or support needs we should plan for?  
   ______

6. Are you directly involved in building Flux or this portfolio prototype?
   - [ ] Yes
   - [ ] No

## Selection logic

**DIRECT_USER include when:**
- has recent recurring responsibility for preparing, reviewing, approving, monitoring or recovering business payments; and
- uses a business-banking/ERP/AP/treasury/payment workflow in real work.

**PROXY include when:**
- has adjacent operational approval/compliance/accounting experience that can inform the workflow but does not match the primary payment context.

**Exclude when:**
- no relevant operational experience;
- directly involved in building Flux;
- cannot consent to anonymized research notes.

Do not promote PROXY participants into DIRECT_USER counts.

## Consent script

Before the task, confirm:

- [ ] I understand Flux is a portfolio prototype using simulated data, not a real bank/payment service.
- [ ] I consent to participating in this study.
- [ ] I consent to anonymized notes about my actions and comments being used to improve the prototype and document the case study.
- [ ] I understand no real financial credentials, account details, customer data or payment information should be entered or shared.
- [ ] I know I may stop at any time.

## Data handling

Collect only the anonymized participant ID, eligibility class, session method, task notes, confidence and relevant experience band. Do not commit names, emails, phone numbers, employer-sensitive data, real account details or screenshots containing private financial information to the repository.
