System foundations

Precise by default.
Calm about money.

Flux is for the people in a small company who ask for money, approve it and pay it out. The system puts amounts, rules and the next approver first, and keeps decoration out of the way.

DirectionPrecise / Institutional / Efficient
Primary usersTeam lead and accountant
SignatureApproval Rail

01.02

Typography

IBM Plex Sans carries the interface and people's names, and covers Vietnamese. IBM Plex Mono with tabular numbers is for amounts, IDs, dates and rule thresholds.

01.03

Spacing, grid and geometry

Compact spacing keeps a week of requests on one screen. Square edges and hairline borders read like a ledger, not a consumer app.

Geometry

0–2px radius

No glass cards, glow or soft shadows. Borders carry grouping and state.

Layout

220px sidebar, ledger list on phones

On desktop the sidebar stays put while requests use a table and a detail pane. On phones each request becomes two lines.

01.06

Motion principles

Motion explains a change of state. It never competes with the amounts.

Purpose

Clarify change

Use motion for selection, the detail pane, a step moving along the rail and a saved change.

Timing

Fast and functional

Short transitions around 120–220ms with no decorative overshoot.

Accessibility

Reduced motion

Keep every state visible when prefers-reduced-motion is on.
