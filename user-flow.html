Critical journey

Get the money without chasing anyone.

The hero flow follows a vendor payment large enough to need the director. Lan, Khoa, Bảo and Hà should each see where the request is and what they have to do, from the request to the payout.

TriggerVND 45,000,000 vendor payment
RuleTeam lead → Director
End statePaid, with a full trail
04.01

Hero flow — trade fair brochures

The normal path favors certainty over fewer clicks. Everyone should know why each approval exists and who acts next.

01 / EMPLOYEECreate request

Lan picks Vendor payment, Phương Nam Printing, the amount and the purpose.

02 / SYSTEMApply the rules

Above VND 20,000,000, Rule 01 adds the director. The review step shows the chain.

03 / TEAM LEADKhoa approves

He sees the purpose, vendor, amount and rule before deciding.

04 / DIRECTORBảo approves

Khoa's approval is visible as part of the request.

05 / ACCOUNTANTHà pays out

The request waits at Payout until the accountant pays it with the request reference.

06 / SYSTEMPaid and traced

The request is completed and the activity trail keeps every step.

04.03

Exception branches

Recovery matters as much as the happy path when money is involved.

REJECTED

An approver rejects

A reason is required. Lan sees who rejected it, why, and that nothing was debited. Designed next: submitting again starts a new cycle instead of overwriting the old one.

CANCELLED

Lan cancels while pending

Only the requester can cancel, and only while the request is pending. It stays in the list under Rejected or cancelled.

FAILED

The payout fails

Keep approval and payout apart. Say that no money left the account and keep the approvals, so Hà can retry without a second request.

04.04

Second flow — settling an advance

An advance is money given before it is spent. The flow is only finished when the receipts are in.

01Advance approved

VND 26,000,000 for the Đà Nẵng trade fair (REQ-2038) is reserved for Lan.

02Add receipts

She adds the flights, the hotel and the rides, each linked to the advance.

03Missing receipt flagged

A claimed amount without a file shows as Missing receipt.

04Accountant reviews

Hà approves each receipt or rejects it with a reason.

05Trail kept

Every receipt stays linked to the advance it settles.

04.05

Permission flow

Role boundaries name who acts next instead of only disabling buttons.

Employee

Can ask, cannot approve

After submitting, the decision buttons are replaced by who approves next and the rule.

Team lead

First approval

Approves or rejects requests from the team, with the rule and amount in view.

Director

Large requests

Receives requests above VND 20,000,000 with the team lead's decision already visible.
