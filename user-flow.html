Critical journey

Move money without losing control.

The hero flow follows a cross-border payment that crosses a policy threshold. The experience must make the control chain visible to the initiator, the manager and the CFO from creation through execution.

04.01

Hero flow — international payment

The normal path optimizes confidence rather than raw click count. Users should know why each approval step exists and who owns the next action.

01 / EMPLOYEECreate payment

Select beneficiary, currency, amount and purpose.

02 / SYSTEMEvaluate policy

Amount and currency trigger Cross-border policy 01.

03 / MANAGERReview evidence

Inspect counterparty, purpose and supporting data.

04 / MANAGERApprove stage 1

Decision is recorded and CFO becomes next owner.

05 / CFOApprove stage 2

Final control check releases the payment.

06 / SYSTEMExecute + trace

Settlement begins and the full activity trail remains visible.

04.03

Exception branches

High-consequence finance UX is defined as much by recovery as by the happy path.

REJECTED

Manager or CFO rejects

Capture an explicit reason. The payment returns to the initiator with evidence and history intact. Editing creates a new submission event, not a silent overwrite.

EXPIRED

Approval window expires

Mark the request expired, name the policy window that elapsed, and provide a controlled replacement path instead of auto-resubmitting.

FAILED

Settlement fails

Separate approval success from settlement success. Explain the bank or beneficiary failure and preserve the already-completed approval evidence.

04.04

Recovery flow — rejected payment

Rejection is not a dead end. The system needs a controlled edit-and-resubmit path that protects audit history.

01Reject with reason

Reviewer records why the payment cannot proceed.

02Notify initiator

Reason and required correction appear in the payment detail.

03Edit permitted fields

Initiator changes bank data, evidence or purpose while prior state stays in history.

04Re-evaluate policy

System recalculates the control chain using the edited transaction.

05Resubmit

A new approval cycle begins with a linked audit event.

04.05

Permission flow

Role boundaries should explain ownership rather than merely disable controls.

Employee

Can create, cannot self-approve

After submission, decision controls are replaced with current owner and policy status.

Finance Manager

Stage-one authority

Can approve within policy boundaries and escalate when required evidence is insufficient.

CFO

Final high-threshold authority

Receives already-reviewed transactions with the manager decision visible as evidence.
