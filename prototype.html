05.02

Main prototype scenarios

The prototype should be evaluated through complete financial decisions rather than isolated screens.

Flow A · hero

Create → Review → Approve → Execute

An employee initiates a high-threshold cross-border transfer. Policy triggers dual approval. Finance Manager reviews first, CFO reviews second, then the payment moves to execution with an audit trail.

Explore payment workspaceView flow logic

Flow B · governance

Policy → Trigger → Decision context

An admin defines an IF/THEN approval rule. The product exposes that rule wherever it affects a transaction so reviewers understand why the control chain exists.

Explore approval policies

Flow C · recovery

Reject → Correct → Re-evaluate → Resubmit

A reviewer rejects a payment with a reason. The initiator corrects permitted data, policy is re-evaluated, and a new approval cycle begins without erasing the previous decision history.

Review recovery flow

Flow D · role boundary

Employee ↔ Approver

Role switching demonstrates that shared context remains visible while decision rights change. Users should understand who can act next without encountering unexplained disabled controls.

Test role switcher
05.03

Prototype fidelity

This screen distinguishes implemented behavior from product assumptions so the prototype does not imply production evidence it does not have.

LayerCurrent prototype evidenceBoundary
Visual systemImplemented tokens, typography, navigation shell, tables, detail panes, status language and Approval Rail.Browser-rendered UI, not a production design-system package.
InteractionRole switching, filters, detail selection, payment interaction and motion behaviors are represented in the static frontend.No real banking network, ledger or backend state.
DataRealistic currencies, counterparties, amounts, timestamps and approval scenarios.All operational data is simulated.
Product outcomeFlows are designed around visibility, control and decision confidence.No measured business impact or usability result is claimed here.
05.04

Review checklist

A focused way to inspect the prototype as a recruiter, designer or product partner.

01 / PRODUCT

Can you explain the control chain?

Open Payments and identify why the selected transfer needs approval, who owns the current step and what comes next.

02 / SYSTEM

Do states stay consistent?

Compare pending, completed, failed and permission states across the working product and component inventory.

03 / RECOVERY

Does failure preserve context?

Use the user-flow documentation to inspect rejection and settlement-failure recovery instead of evaluating only the happy path.
