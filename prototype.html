05.03

Prototype fidelity

What is built, and what is only assumed, so the prototype does not imply evidence it does not have.

LayerBuiltBoundary
Visual systemTokens, type, the shell, tables, detail panes, status language and the Approval Rail.Rendered in the browser, not a packaged design system.
InteractionCreate, approve, reject and cancel requests; receipts; transfers; team roles; rules; 17 browser flow tests.No backend, bank connection or email. Data lives in the browser tab. The accountant's payout, a failed payout and expiry are shown as states, not actions; the last two only in the design lens. Submitting a rejected request again is designed, not built.
DataA fictional 40-person company with VND amounts, local vendors and named people.Every company, person and amount is made up.
OutcomeFlows are designed so people can see who approves next and what is still owed.No user has tested it yet; no time saved or error reduced is claimed.
05.04

Review checklist

A quick way to inspect the prototype as a recruiter, designer or product partner.

01 / PRODUCT

Can you say who approves next?

Open Requests, pick the brochures request and say why the director is involved and who acts now.

02 / SYSTEM

Do states stay consistent?

Compare pending, completed, rejected and receipt states in the product and in the state inventory.

03 / RECOVERY

Does failure keep the context?

Follow the rejection, failed payout and missing receipt paths, not only the happy path.
